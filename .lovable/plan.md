# Social Media Marketing Hub Rebuild

## Goal
Rebuild the existing `/social-media-marketing-sri-lanka` page into BuzzConnect’s definitive commercial social media hub without changing its URL. The page will guide visitors from business problem to strategy, platform choice, campaign delivery, pricing, proof of capability, and enquiry.

## What will change
- Replace the current generic opening with the approved outcome-led headline, LKR 50,000 starting price, two primary actions, and concise service signals.
- Reorganize the page into a decision journey: problem → definition → BuzzConnect framework → platform selection → services → campaign process → outcomes → pricing → industries/examples → differentiators → agency checklist → FAQs → enquiry.
- Add the requested practical sections, including:
  - “Is your social media actually working?” diagnosis
  - strategy-to-measurement funnel
  - Attract / Engage / Capture / Nurture / Convert / Measure / Optimise framework
  - Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn comparison
  - organic versus paid guidance
  - vanity metrics versus business outcomes
  - seven-step campaign process and concrete deliverables
  - industry-specific opportunities and clearly labelled hypothetical campaign examples
  - Findit.lk and multi-channel ecosystem
  - campaign readiness, budget, agency-selection, and “what we don’t believe in” checklists
- Keep the packages page as the pricing source of truth. Clearly separate BuzzConnect’s agency fee from advertising media spend everywhere.
- Add multiple contextual actions, including a short campaign enquiry form that opens WhatsApp and records the enquiry through the existing tracking flow.
- Use only verified first-party facts. Remove unsupported superlatives, absolute outcome claims, unsourced audience figures, fake comparisons, and implied guarantees.
- Keep the 2026 guide educational and link to it contextually instead of duplicating its market-reference content.

## SEO and internal linking
- Use the approved title, unique description, canonical URL, one H1, visible breadcrumbs, indexable text, and accurate Service, BreadcrumbList, and visible FAQ schema.
- Link contextually to existing Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, advertising, management, campaign, lead-generation, packages, cost, multi-channel, and lead-generation pages.
- Include only routes that exist; do not create extra pages or doorway URLs as part of this rebuild.
- Preserve the existing sitemap entry and static HTML generation for search crawlers.

## Design and interaction
- Preserve the current BuzzConnect design system while making the page feel like a focused agency playbook rather than a long generic article.
- Use compact diagrams, comparison tables, process steps, metric contrasts, and restrained motion.
- Keep essential information as text, not embedded in imagery.
- Ensure buttons use the existing design components and all WhatsApp actions retain service attribution.

## Verification
- Check TypeScript and the targeted test suite.
- Verify the live preview on desktop and mobile for one H1, no horizontal overflow, readable tables, working specialist links, visible FAQs, accurate pricing language, and functional enquiry/WhatsApp actions.
- Verify the generated static page contains the intended title, description, canonical, H1, and crawlable supporting content.
