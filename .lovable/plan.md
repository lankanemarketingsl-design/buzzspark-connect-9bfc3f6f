# Brand Blast 360 Industry Section Redesign

## Goal
Turn the “Who it’s for” section into a conversion-focused industry explorer that helps marketers immediately understand what they can promote and how 988,000+ people are reached.

## Changes
- Replace the long all-industries view with a focused industry selector; show one industry’s campaign ideas at a time.
- Add a prominent visual reach panel with people icons, the 988,000+ total, channel contribution bar, and all five channel totals.
- Present industry-specific campaign ideas in polished, scannable cards using the existing content.
- Add an industry-aware WhatsApp action with the chosen industry named in the message.
- Keep the existing page branding, mobile behavior, WhatsApp number, and admin click tracking.

## Technical details
- Update only the Brand Blast 360 page presentation and interaction.
- Reuse `bb360UseCases` as the content source; no campaign claims or prices will be invented.
- Use existing semantic theme tokens and shared button styles.
- Verify the redesigned section at desktop and mobile sizes, including switching industries and opening the correct WhatsApp link.
