# Brand Blast 360 Industry Page Redesign

## Goal
Redesign the isolated Brand Blast 360 industry landing pages as premium, promotion-focused sales pages while preserving the search intent and content of every existing industry-marketing page.

## Initial scope
- Build the Hotels page first at `/brand-blast-360/hotels`.
- Reuse the existing Brand Blast 360 packages, prices, channels, WhatsApp routing, and analytics.
- Keep `/hotel-marketing-sri-lanka` and all other existing pages unchanged.
- Once the Hotels architecture is verified, extend the same reusable structure with genuinely unique content to the other 10 existing Brand Blast 360 industry routes.
